# Public-domain Jyotish texts for astrology rules (Phase 3.6)

**Research date:** 2026-09-30 (web research by an AI agent; nothing downloaded). **Status:** candidates for the owner's decision; no rules extracted yet.

**Bar used:** public domain in both countries.
- **USA:** published in 1930 or earlier (95-year term; this also covers Indian works restored in 1996). 1931 works become PD on 1 Jan 2027.
- **India:** author or translator died in 1965 or earlier (life + 60).
- archive.org "Public Domain" labels on Digital Library of India uploads are hints only, not evidence.

## Recommended shortlist

- **Dasha-lord rules:** J4, then J5 (check OCR and completeness), then J1 ch. 8 (flag each rule as non-Vimshottari).
- **Planet-in-house rules:** J1 ch. 20, cross-checked with J2 and J3.

## Excluded or not yet public domain

- **Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra:** no pre-1930 English translation exists. Excluded translations: Krishna Rau & Choudhari (1963), Santhanam (1984), G.C. Sharma.
- **Saravali:** Krishna Rau & Choudhari (1961) and Santhanam (1983) are copyrighted. The 1907 edition is Sanskrit only.
- **V. Subrahmanya Sastri** (Jataka Parijata 1932–33, Phaladeepika 1937/1950, Brihat Jataka 2nd ed.): not PD in the US until 2028 or later, and his death year is unknown.
- **Rao's *Stri Jataka* (1931):** PD in the US from 1 Jan 2027. Rao's posthumous 1946–55 editions are excluded.
- **Not relevant or not usable:** Brihat Samhita (Chidambaram Iyer 1884) is PD but covers omens, not horoscopy. Neely 2007 (on wisdomlib) is copyrighted, so do not quote wisdomlib. B.V. Raman's editions are copyrighted.

## Rule constraints (same as palm rules)

- **Verbatim quote and locator** for every rule, checked by lint against the downloaded text (as B1 is for Cheiro).
- **No health, lifespan, death (maraka), disease or fertility statements** (D-009). Classical texts contain many; the extractor must drop them.
- **Every rule states its dasa system.** Brihat Jataka dasas must not be silently mapped onto Vimshottari.
